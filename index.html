The mechanism: The Autoloop

The six-stage pattern most people never see, because it happens fast and mostly outside conscious awareness:

1

Spark

An internal state or cue — boredom, loneliness, stress, an empty stretch of time.

2

Pull

The Spark turns into a craving, fast enough to feel like a pull, not a thought.

3

Script

A pre-loaded sequence runs — unlock, open, search. The "I wasn't deciding" part.

4

Discharge

The act itself — the point every willpower method tries and mostly fails to stop.

5

Numb

A short window of flatness — not pleasure, more a dulling of the original state.

6

Reload

Guilt sets in — and quietly becomes the next Spark.

That last step is the trap. The guilt doesn't end the cycle — it feeds it. That's why the pattern tightens instead of fading.
